I was checking out some syntax on [dokuwiki's website](http://www.dokuwiki.org/dokuwiki) and suddenly a duck appeared from the right hand side. When I hovered over it, I got a web browser crosshair. When I clicked on it (_bang_), a bullet hole image appeared in the web page and the duck (_quack_) fell to the bottom of the browser window.

Duck hunt!

I didn't see it blogged about yet so there ya go. Techcrunch has [a good wrap-up](http://techcrunch.com/april-fools-shenanigans/) of 2010 april fool's jokes. This year didn't seem as big as last year's.
